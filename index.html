Research Interests

Robots take many forms, because each is configured for the purpose it serves and the problem it is built to solve. I think their capabilities are used far more effectively when such robots form a team and carry out multiple missions under human supervision, rather than when each one excels at a single task in isolation. My interests therefore center on multi-robot systems, together with the robotics research that makes each member of such a team capable on its own.

Projects

Aerial Manipulation

TALON

A simulated quadrotor whose landing gear doubles as a gripper. A four-phase pick-and-deliver pipeline with PPO and PID controllers, analyzed through grasp depth.
Aerial Platform

Soltrone

A quadrotor built from components, running ArduPilot with a Raspberry Pi 5 companion computer, as the platform for onboard perception work.
Reinforcement Learning

Agent Mcqueen

Autonomous racing in the F1tenth simulator: a PPO policy trained on 450 randomized tracks, extended to head-to-head overtaking by residual learning against a frozen expert.
Edge Vision

Herbot

A plant-care robot that classifies herb species and leaf disease on a Coral Edge TPU and removes diseased leaves with a rail-mounted gripper.
